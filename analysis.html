8. Balance Suggestions Summary (No Gold Cost Changes)

Tide's Heart (T1): +200 HP → +325 HP
Currently 1.8-2.4× worse than Reef Shield per gold at all levels. +325 HP makes it ~equal at Lv3 (where raw HP matters most) while armor naturally outscales at Lv8 (armor multiplies with growing HP pool). On Granitacles (highest VIT), the HP has even more value due to Colossus/Smash % scaling.
Regen Spines (T1): +3 HP Regen → +5 HP Regen
At 3/s, regen contributes 0.03s extra TTD under 36 supers — essentially zero. At +5/s it's still small (0.05s) but scales better into Super Regen Spines T2. The upgrade path needs the T1 to feel meaningful.
Super Regen Spines (T2): Add +150 HP alongside the +12 regen
Currently worst T2 defensive under burst (0.0007-0.0019/g). Adding 150 HP gives a buffer so the regen has time to work. New TTD/g would be ~0.0020-0.0035, comparable to Heart of the Sea.
Casque of Valor (T2): +200 HP → +350 HP
At same cost as Reef Armor (335g), Casque gives half the survivability. +350 HP makes the HP path competitive at Lv3 (where each point of HP is worth more of your total pool), while Reef Armor's armor+Tidal Fortitude still wins at Lv8.
Enchantress' Fluid (T2): Abysmal Drink: 300 HP heal → 500 HP heal
At 375g it's the most expensive T2 defensive but gives less TTD than the 335g Casque. Stronger heal makes the active ability feel impactful — 500 HP / 60s = 8.3 HP/s avg, up from 5.
Runic Axe (T2): Runic Mark: 35 → 45 magic damage
Wall DPS/gold is 0.09/g — weakest T2 offensive by far. At 45 magic, it reaches ~0.11/g, competitive with Gauntlets and below Tridents. Magic damage ignoring wall armor is the item's identity — it should feel worth the 260g.
Voodoo Doll (T2): Voodoo Decay: −3 → −4 armor
Personal wall DPS is very low (0.024/g). The value is team-wide — −4 armor on walls (15→11) gives +13.7% phys to ALL allies vs −3 giving +10.5%. Makes the item's team identity clearer.
Foreteller's Sickle (T3): Runic Wrath base: 3% → 4% max mana
Base 3% on Lucidious Lv6 = 47/hit — unimpressive for an 860g T3. At 4%: 63/hit base, 126/hit with buff. On Glacious Lv8: 90/hit base, 180/hit buff. Creates clear WIS-scaling identity where Glacious gets ~25% more value than Lucidious.
Spear of Fervor (T3): Add Lifesteal: 20 + 25% vitality
V5 removed lifesteal and all AD, leaving it as pure AS. It needs a secondary identity. Lifesteal gives sustained tower-diving power: at Lv8 Lucidious = 20+17.6 = 37.6 HP/hit. At Granitacles: 20+20.8 = 40.8 HP/hit — VIT-scaling sustain.
Helmet of the Damned (T3): Add +5 AD to base stats (+30 → +35 AD)
Currently 765g for +30 AD, +25% AS, and ward-based utility. Personal wall DPS is below Poseidon and Mace. +5 AD gives a small direct buff without changing the ward-centric identity.
